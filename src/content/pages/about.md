## The short version

I'm a computer vision and robotics engineer based in the Bay Area. I also make ceramics under [Spells and Bristles](https://instagram.com/spells.and.bristles) and write about whatever my restless brain won't let go of.

## How I got here

I studied Electrical and Electronics Engineering at Bangalore Institute of Technology, then moved to Philadelphia for a Master's in Robotics at the University of Pennsylvania. That's where I fell in love with the messy, uncertain side of making machines perceive the real world.

After Penn, I joined Navisens — a four-person startup in San Francisco where I worked on sensor fusion and magnetometer-based localization for mobile devices. Small team, every problem was yours.

From there I went to Meta, where I became a key contributor to the inside-out tracking system that powers 6DoF VR on the Quest and Quest 2. I worked on controller tracking, headset localization, bundle adjustment, and loop closure — the kind of work where a millimeter matters. Some of that work was featured on the Oculus Developer Blog, and I filed a patent for pre-scanning and indexing nearby objects during load.

I then moved to Nuro to work on semantic mapping for autonomous vehicles, building ML annotation tools and sign detection pipelines. Most recently, I was a Senior Computer Vision Engineer at FarmWise Labs, working on robotic weeding for agriculture — visual odometry, crop detection, trajectory planning, and resimulation tools.

## Spells and Bristles

It started with painting. I made a calendar of my paintings and sold it on Etsy — that was the first version of Spells and Bristles. A couple of years later I got into pottery and have been hooked ever since. Clay is the perfect counterweight to code: immediate, tactile, and unforgiving in a totally different way. Fire doesn't accept pull requests.

Follow the work on [Instagram](https://instagram.com/spells.and.bristles).

## Writing

I write because I have a restless, overthinking brain that needs an outlet. Some of it is technical, some philosophical, some poetic. I'm not trying to stay in a lane — I write about whatever I'm trying to figure out.

## Beyond work

I've served as a committee member and panelist at the Grace Hopper Conference, designed interactive museum exhibits at CuriOdyssey, mentored elementary students through STEMCorps, and taught coding fundamentals at StreetCode Academy.
